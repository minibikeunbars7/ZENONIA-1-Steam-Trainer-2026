## 🎮 About the Game

ZENONIA 1 is a single-player action role-playing game originally developed and published by Gamevil. It first launched in South Korea on August 28, 2008, followed by international releases on iOS in 2009 and Android in 2010. The restored PC version was released on Steam on August 31, 2026, with Com2uS Holdings and CFK handling the modern release.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **ZENONIA 1**. This trainer provides optional advantages for ZENONIA 1's real-time combat, character progression, exploration, inventory management, hunger system, equipment durability, and in-game economy. It is designed for the single-player campaign and can be enabled selectively to preserve the original action RPG experience.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to reduce grinding, experiment with Warrior, Paladin, and Assassin builds, compatibility hunger and repair costs, test skills freely, or replay the story with a more relaxed progression curve.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

ZENONIA 1 uses direct four-directional movement for exploring towns, fields, and dungeons. Regret can also use a forward dash during traversal and combat. The trainer does not replace normal movement controls, but removing combat pressure and resource limits makes it easier to travel through dangerous areas.

### Tools & Inventory

The inventory contains weapons, armor, accessories, consumables, quest items, and magic items. Equipment has class, level, and attribute requirements, while worn gear loses durability over time. Gold can be used for shopping and repairs, and magic items can be combined to create stronger results. Use inventory-related options carefully when changing large quantities of items.

### World & Environment

The campaign follows Regret through a large fantasy world filled with towns, fields, dungeons, enemies, NPCs, and quests. Up to five quests can be held at once, and some objectives depend on the time of day. Story decisions can move Regret toward good or evil alignment and alter parts of the journey.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat takes place in real time against groups of enemies and bosses. Choose one of three classes: Warrior for balanced melee power, Paladin for defense and healing abilities, or Assassin for speed and high damage. Normal attacks, class skills, passive abilities, equipment bonuses, HP, and SP all contribute to combat performance.
